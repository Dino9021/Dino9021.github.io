雲端同步的間隔與上限

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-07

正體中文

一句話

同步不是每秒都在跑。它有幾個間隔與一個每日上限，目的是省你的行動數據與電池，順便避免 Google 因為請求太頻繁而暫時擋住你的帳號。

目前的數值

項目數值為什麼
有東西要上傳時，最短間隔 30 秒 連續翻頁會一直更新進度。等 30 秒再一次傳完，比每翻一頁就連一次網省得多。
去看別台有沒有變動，最短間隔 5 分鐘 這個一定要連網才知道結果，而多數時候答案是「沒變」。5 分鐘一次已經比人換裝置的速度快。
失敗後重試 1 → 2 → 4 … 最多 32 分鐘 沒訊號時連環重試最耗電。每失敗一次就等久一點，並加上一點隨機偏移，避免你的多台裝置同時醒來一起打。
一天最多同步 1000 次 正常使用一天大概幾十次。1000 是留了很大餘裕的上限，不是設計來限制你的。

什麼時候會馬上同步

  • 你按下〔立即同步〕—— 手動一律照做，不看上面任何間隔。
  • 回到書櫃（關書、切回 App 都會經過這裡）。
  • 匯入書籍的掃描剛跑完 —— 那是一批新書第一次可以同步的時刻，會跳過最短間隔。

撞到每日上限會怎樣

  • 你按〔立即同步〕還是會同步。上限只擋「你沒要求的那些背景往返」——你親手按下按鈕就是明確表達「我現在就要」，那一律照做。
  • 自動同步暫停到隔天，你的資料不會有任何損失，兩邊都原封不動留著。
  • 隔天自動歸零，不必做任何事。
  • 你可以在〔全域設定 → 儲存 → 雲端同步〕看到今天用了幾次、還剩幾次。

常見誤解

  • 「上限是為了省 Google 的配額吧？」不是。Google 給每個使用者的額度是每分鐘 325,000 單位，而一次同步約 350 單位 —— 一天同步 1000 次也只用掉那個額度的萬分之 0.7。配額從來不是限制因素。
  • 「間隔會不會讓我的進度掉？」不會。改動會先記在你的裝置上，等下一次同步一起送出去。間隔只影響「多久之後送出」，不影響「會不會送出」。
  • 「上傳失敗是不是資料就沒了？」不會。失敗時兩邊的資料都維持原狀，只是這一趟沒成功，下一趟會再試。

會上傳什麼

  • 只有你產生的資料：閱讀進度、書籤、畫重點、每本書的設定、書架。
  • 絕不上傳書本檔案。同步的檔案裡連書名、作者都沒有 —— 那些匯入書就能得到，沒必要放到雲端。
  • 每本書一個小小的 JSON 檔，通常只有幾 KB。

English

In one line

Sync does not run every second. A few minimum intervals and one daily ceiling keep it from wasting your mobile data and battery, and stop Google from temporarily blocking your account for making requests too often.

Current values

SettingValueWhy
Minimum gap when there is something to upload 30 seconds Turning pages keeps updating your position. Waiting 30 seconds and sending once costs far less than one connection per page.
Minimum gap before checking other devices 5 minutes This needs the network just to learn the answer, and the answer is usually "nothing changed". Every 5 minutes is already faster than people switch devices.
Retry after a failure 1 → 2 → 4 … up to 32 minutes Retrying in a tight loop with no signal is the fastest way to drain a battery. Each failure waits longer, with a little random offset so your devices don't all wake at once.
Maximum syncs per day 1000 Ordinary use is a few dozen a day. 1000 is a ceiling with a lot of headroom, not a restriction aimed at you.

When it syncs immediately

  • You tap Sync now — a manual sync always runs and ignores every interval above.
  • You return to the library (closing a book or switching back to the app both pass through it).
  • An import scan just finished — that is the first moment a batch of new books can sync, so it skips the minimum gap.

What happens at the daily ceiling

  • Tapping Sync now still works. The ceiling only holds back background round-trips you did not ask for; pressing the button yourself always runs.
  • Automatic sync pauses until tomorrow. Nothing is lost; both sides keep their data untouched.
  • It resets by itself the next day. You don't have to do anything.
  • Settings → Storage → Cloud sync shows how many you have used today and how many are left.

Common misunderstandings

  • "Isn't the limit there to save Google's quota?" No. Google allows 325,000 units per minute per user, and one sync costs about 350 — a thousand syncs a day uses roughly 0.007% of it. Quota was never the constraint.
  • "Could an interval lose my progress?" No. Changes are saved on your device first and travel with the next sync. Intervals affect when data is sent, never whether.
  • "If an upload fails, is the data gone?" No. On failure both sides stay exactly as they were; only that attempt failed, and the next one retries.

What gets uploaded

  • Only what you created: reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, per-book settings, shelves.
  • Never your book files. The synced files don't even contain titles or authors — importing a book gives you those, so there is no reason to put them in the cloud.
  • One small JSON file per book, usually a few kilobytes.
