廣告（Google AdMob）

廣告出現在哪裡

  • 畫面最下方的橫幅廣告。
  • 每進入遊戲幾次（大約第 6 到第 10 次）之後，等下一局自然結束、結果出現在畫面上時，會先顯示「廣告即將開始」約 1.5 秒，接著出現一則全螢幕廣告；關掉之後才出現〔再玩一局〕這類按鈕。在那之前你點一下畫面、按返回鍵或離開 App，這一次就不出現（延到之後的一局）。進入遊戲的那一刻不會有廣告；情報員靠邊站、海龜湯沒有這種廣告。
  • App 放到背景超過 60 秒、再回到 App 時，可能出現一則全螢幕廣告（最多 4 小時一次；剛打開 App 的時候不會出現）。
  • 你在皇冠頁自己按〔看一則廣告，得 1 點〕時，會播一則影片廣告；看完就得到 1 點，每天最多 10 次。不按就不會播。
  • 你在皇冠頁進〔🎡 幸運轉盤〕、按〔看廣告轉一次〕時，會播一則影片廣告（和上面同一種）；看完轉盤才轉，一天最多轉 3 次。這些廣告不算進上面那 10 次，也不另外給那 1 點。不按就不會播。
  • 你用點數換到「沒有全螢幕廣告」的時段裡，上面兩種全螢幕廣告都不會出現；橫幅廣告照常顯示。
  • 買了標準包，或在限時體驗包的期限內：開屏、插頁與橫幅廣告都不出現；你自己按的獎勵廣告（看廣告得點、幸運轉盤）照常可以看，廣告程式也照常啟動，所以下面〈Google 的廣告程式會收集什麼〉對你仍然適用。

你可以怎麼控制

  • 也可以到手機的「設定 → 應用程式 → 口袋派對 → 儲存空間 → 清除資料」，你的同意選擇會一起清掉，下次開 App 時，需要的話會再問你一次。這也會清掉 App 存在你手機裡的全部資料，包括你的點數（見〈這個 App 在你手機裡存了什麼〉）。
  • 沒有網路時，遊戲照樣能玩。

朗讀功能

多個遊戲可以讓 App 把文字唸出來。這一段請看仔細：

  • 朗讀使用的是你手機本身的語音引擎（Android 系統的文字轉語音功能），不是我們的服務，也不是任何第三方服務。
  • 被唸的文字，是這個 App 內建的遊戲內容，不是你的個人資料。
  • 但要老實說：我們把文字交給你手機上選定的語音引擎之後，那段文字怎麼被處理，就由那個引擎決定。某些語音引擎（例如 Google 的）可能會把文字送到雲端合成。那不在我們的掌控範圍內，適用的是那個引擎自己的隱私權政策。你可以在手機的「設定 → 系統 → 語言與輸入 → 文字轉語音」裡看到目前用的是哪一個，也可以換成完全離線的引擎。
  • 為了讓朗讀在你關掉螢幕之後還能唸完，App 會啟動一個前景服務，並在朗讀期間讓手機保持喚醒。這些只用在朗讀，不收集、也不傳送任何資料。
  • 朗讀不使用麥克風。文字轉語音是把字唸出來，不是聽你說話。這個 App 從來沒有要求過麥克風權限，你可以在系統的權限頁面自己確認。

付款完全由 Google Play 處理，App 與開發者不會收到、也不會儲存你的付款資料（卡號等）。App 只在你的手機裡記錄你買過什麼、限時體驗包是什麼時候買的，並向 Google Play 查詢來確認、還原你的購買（換手機或重新安裝時，用同一個 Google 帳號就會還原）；查詢不經過開發者的任何伺服器（本 App 沒有自己的伺服器）。退款之後，下次連上 Google Play 時會回到沒有購買的狀態；限時體驗包到期就結束。

App 裡的點數只能用開 App、玩遊戲、看廣告（包括幸運轉盤）賺，不能用錢買，也不能換成錢。

English

The developer collects no data about you. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer runs no server.

The app shows Google AdMob ads: to serve them, Google's ad code collects the data listed under "Ads" below and handles it under Google's own policies.

In-app purchases are paid through Google Play; neither the app nor the developer gets your payment details (see "Purchases").

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer runs no server, and the app never sends your settings, play history, or anything you type to the developer.

Specifically, the developer does not receive any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Contacts, photos, videos, files, or calendar
  • Microphone or camera
  • Your settings, play history, ratings, or quiz password

Data collected by the ad code is received and handled by Google; see the next section.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Where ads appear

  • A banner ad along the bottom of the screen.
  • After every few game entries (roughly the 6th to the 10th), when a round next ends naturally and its result is on screen, an "Ad starting soon" notice shows for about 1.5 seconds and then a full-screen ad appears; the next-step buttons (such as Play again) appear after you close it. If you tap the screen, press Back or leave the app before that, it is skipped and moved to a later round. No ad appears when you enter a game, and two of the games (情報員靠邊站, 海龜湯) never show this ad.
  • When you come back to the app after it has been in the background for more than 60 seconds, a full-screen ad may appear (at most once every 4 hours; never right when you first open the app).
  • When you yourself tap the "watch an ad, get 1 point" button (看一則廣告，得 1 點) on the crown page, a video ad plays; watch it to the end and you get 1 point, up to 10 times a day. If you don't tap it, it never plays.
  • When you open the Lucky Spinner (🎡 幸運轉盤) from the crown page and tap "watch an ad to spin" (看廣告轉一次), a video ad of the same kind plays; the wheel spins only after you watch it to the end, up to 3 spins a day. These ads do not count toward the 10 above and do not give that 1 point. If you don't tap it, it never plays.
  • During a "no full-screen ads" period that you got with points, neither kind of full-screen ad above appears; the banner ad still shows.
  • If you own the standard pack, or during a trial pack: no app-open, interstitial or banner ads appear. Rewarded ads that you tap for yourself (watch an ad for a point, the Lucky Spinner) are still available and the ad code still starts, so "What Google's ad code collects" below still applies to you.

What Google's ad code collects (as published by Google)

  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address. This is not GPS; the app has no location permission.
  • App interactions: for example app launches, taps, and video ad views
  • Diagnostics: performance of the app and the ad code, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy use

It is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit with TLS.

  • Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Regions where consent is required (for example the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland)

What you can control

Network

  • The games themselves need no network. All question banks ship inside the install file, so everything works offline.
  • The network is used for two things: Google's ad code connects to Google's servers to load ads; for in-app purchases the app asks Google Play to confirm and restore your purchases (Google Play's purchase components also connect to Google).
  • The app does not connect to any server of the developer's; the developer has none.

Text-to-speech

Several games can read text aloud. Please read this part carefully:

  • Speech uses your phone's own speech engine (Android text-to-speech), not a service of ours and not a third-party service.
  • The text read aloud is game content built into the app. It is not your personal data.
  • To be honest about the limit: once we hand the text to the speech engine you selected, what happens to that text is up to that engine. Some engines (Google's, for example) may send text to the cloud to synthesize it. That is outside our control, and that engine's own privacy policy applies. You can see which engine you use, and switch to a fully offline one, under Settings → System → Languages & input → Text-to-speech.
  • So that reading can finish after you turn the screen off, the app starts a foreground service and keeps the phone awake while it reads. These are used only for reading aloud and collect and send no data.
