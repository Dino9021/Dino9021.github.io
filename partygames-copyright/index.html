Bundled font subset and its licence

So that the emoji in the App show up on every phone and look the same everywhere (phones of different makes and versions draw emoji differently, and older Android versions lack some and show boxes), the App bundles a glyph subset of Noto Color Emoji — only the emoji the App uses, not the complete font. The developer extracted the subset from the upstream font, so it is a modified version. It is not intended to be installed or redistributed as a font; the complete font is available at https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji.

As required by clause 2 of the SIL Open Font License 1.1, the copyright notice is: Noto Color Emoji — Copyright 2022 Google Inc.
