正體中文

Reboku 是一款電子書閱讀器，支援 EPUB、CBZ、PDF、TXT 與 Markdown(.md)。使用上有任何問題、建議或錯誤回報，歡迎來信:

聯絡信箱:Reboku.app@gmail.com

來信時請附上裝置型號、系統版本，以及 App 的版本號(在 App 的〔資訊〕頁可以看到),能幫助我們更快找到問題。

常見問題

怎麼把書放進來?

在書庫右上角按〔匯入書籍〕,可以選單一檔案，或選一整個資料夾(會依資料夾建立書架)。重複的書會自動合併。支援 EPUB、CBZ、PDF、TXT、Markdown。

買了完整版，換裝置或重裝後怎麼恢復?

用當初購買的同一個商店帳號登入，打開升級畫面(工具列上的皇冠),按〔還原購買〕即可。購買與訂閱都由 App 商店處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料;要取消訂閱，請到商店的訂閱管理頁面操作(升級畫面的〔管理訂閱〕可以直接帶你過去)。

怎麼變更廣告的隱私選擇?

如果你所在的地區法規要求(例如歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士),〔設定〕→〔一般〕裡會有〔開啟隱私選項〕,可以隨時變更或撤回你對廣告資料使用所做的選擇。其他地區不會出現這一項。詳細說明請見隱私權政策。

怎麼備份我的書庫?

在〔設定〕→〔儲存〕→〔備份還原〕按〔匯出備份〕,可以把書庫索引、閱讀進度、書籤、畫重點、設定與書架匯出成一個檔案，之後在任何一台裝置按〔匯入備份〕即可還原。備份不含書本檔案本身,還原後請重新匯入書檔。

English

Reboku is an e-book reader for EPUB, CBZ, PDF, TXT and Markdown (.md). For questions, suggestions or bug reports, please write to us:

Contact: Reboku.app@gmail.com

Please include your device model, OS version and the app version (shown on the app's About page) so we can find the problem faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add books?

Tap Import books at the top right of the library. You can pick a single file or a whole folder (its folders become shelves). Duplicate books are merged automatically. EPUB, CBZ, PDF, TXT and Markdown are supported.

I bought the full version. How do I get it back on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same store account you bought it with, open the upgrade screen (the crown on the toolbar) and tap Restore purchase. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by the app store; we never see your payment details. To cancel a subscription, use the store's subscription settings (the upgrade screen's Manage subscription takes you there).

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Settings → General has Open privacy options, where you can change or withdraw your choices about ad data at any time. Other regions do not show this item. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my library?

In Settings → Storage → Backup, tap Export backup to export the library index, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, settings and shelves to one file, then tap Import backup on any device to restore them. The backup does not contain the book files themselves; import the books again after restoring.
